# Next Planned Changes

This document records upcoming features for the Victorian Empire Trading
Game and notes recently completed items.

## Recently implemented

- **Team-count setup:** The teacher selects 4–8 teams at startup, with
  four selected by default. Each team receives one territory in list
  order; unused territories are hidden and excluded from the round.
- **Phase tracker:** The title, round and year, phase details and action
  button now share one compact tracker bar.
- **Britain's wealth:** A full-width card tracks Britain's balance,
  starting at £25. Britain cannot buy resources it cannot afford, keeping
  its balance at or above zero.
- **Review & Development tax:** Britain collects 20% of each active territory's
  money, rounded down, on entering the phase before development. Tax
  payments are shown on the cards and added to Britain's wealth.
